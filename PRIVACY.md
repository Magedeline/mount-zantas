# Privacy

Mount Zantas does not collect, store or send personal data, and has no analytics or telemetry.

What it does on your computer:
- Looks for Celeste on your own disk (Steam, Epic, GOG, itch app, common folders).
- Reads and writes the `Mods` folder and `blacklist.txt` of the Celeste install you select.
- Keeps its own settings, a small image cache and downloaded tools in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MountZantas`.

What it fetches from the internet, only when you use the related feature:
- Everest, Lönn and Topo releases from GitHub.
- The mod database and news from maddie480.ovh, mod files from celestemodupdater.0x0a.de (or GameBanana when you choose that mirror).
- Mod information and preview images from gamebanana.com.

These services may see your IP address and a `MountZantas/<version>` user-agent, as with any download. This project does not receive that information.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/Magedeline/mount-zantas/issues
